Privacy policy

Make My Space · modular furniture and interiors · last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information Make My Space ("we") collects when you enquire with us, become our customer, work with us as a partner or staff member, or message us on WhatsApp, and how we use it. It covers our website, our WhatsApp Business number, our customer app and our internal project-management system.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile/WhatsApp number, email and site address.
  • Enquiry details: what you are interested in, budget, area, how you heard of us, and enquiries you submit through Facebook or Instagram lead forms.
  • Project information: site measurements and photographs, drawings, material selections, quotations, payments, installation records, service requests and your feedback.
  • Messages: WhatsApp messages you send to our business number, and the delivery status of messages we send you.
  • Login codes: one-time codes sent on WhatsApp to sign in. Codes are not stored after they are sent.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send quotations.
  • To plan, manufacture, deliver and install your furniture, and to keep you updated (for example drawing approvals, installation dates and handover).
  • To record payments and issue receipts and tax invoices.
  • To provide warranty and after-sales service.
  • To send occasional offers on WhatsApp. You can stop these at any time by replying STOP.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for any purpose unrelated to your project.

WhatsApp

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta). Messages are delivered through Meta's infrastructure under WhatsApp's business terms. Service messages about your project are sent because you are our customer or asked us to contact you. Marketing messages are sent only if you have not opted out.

Who can see it

Only our staff who need it for your project can see your information, for example your project manager, designer, factory team and installation team. Access is controlled by role. We share only what is necessary with:

  • the partner (architect, contractor or agent) who referred you, limited to the status of your enquiry;
  • service providers who host our systems and deliver messages (cloud hosting, Meta/WhatsApp);
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep project and invoice records for as long as your warranty runs and as required by tax law. Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted or anonymised after 3 years.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop WhatsApp messages, by writing to hello@makemyspace.in or messaging our WhatsApp number with "DELETE MY DATA". We will confirm within 30 days. Records we must keep by law, such as tax invoices, are retained for the legal period only.

Security

Access is by WhatsApp one-time code with no shared passwords. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and every change to records is logged.
